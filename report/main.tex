\chapter*{General Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{General Introduction}

In an era dominated by social media, content creators and businesses face an increasingly complex challenge: managing multiple social media platforms simultaneously while maintaining high-quality content output. The proliferation of platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook has created a fragmented landscape where creators must juggle content creation, scheduling, analytics monitoring, and account management across numerous interfaces.

The quality of video content has become a critical differentiator in the competitive social media ecosystem. Low-resolution or poorly enhanced videos fail to capture audience attention, yet professional video enhancement tools remain expensive and technically complex for most creators.

This end-of-studies project aims to address these challenges by designing and developing \textbf{BViral Control Center}, a comprehensive, multi-platform social media management dashboard with an integrated AI-powered video enhancement pipeline. The solution provides a centralized interface for scheduling posts, managing connected social media accounts, monitoring analytics, and enhancing video content using state-of-the-art artificial intelligence models.

To present our work, this report is structured into three major chapters:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapter 1 --- State of the Art:} presents the preliminary study, analyzes existing solutions, defines functional and non-functional requirements, and specifies the working methodology.
    \item \textbf{Chapter 2 --- Conceptual Study:} provides the detailed design of our solution using UML diagrams including use case, class, and sequence diagrams.
    \item \textbf{Chapter 3 --- Realisation:} justifies the technology choices, presents the system architecture, and showcases the implemented interfaces.
\end{itemize}

Finally, we conclude with a general conclusion discussing future perspectives and potential improvements.
